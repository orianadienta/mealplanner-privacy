Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android app handles information. The App helps users find recipes and plan a weekly meal schedule (breakfast, lunch, dinner).

1. Personal data we collect

We do not collect personal data. The App has no accounts, login, or registration, does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, or storage permissions, and we do not operate any server that receives data from the App.

2. Data stored on your device

When you add a recipe to your planner, the App stores the following in the device's local storage (a SQLite database):

  • the recipe ID, recipe title, and recipe image URL;
  • the day and meal type (breakfast, lunch, or dinner) you chose.

This data stays on your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else. You can remove it at any time by deleting entries in the App, clearing the App's data in your device settings, or uninstalling the App.

3. Third-party service: TheMealDB

Recipes, categories, and images are loaded from the public service TheMealDB. When you browse recipes, search, choose a category, or open a recipe, the App sends requests to TheMealDB over HTTPS, for example containing a search keyword, a category name, or a recipe ID. As with any internet service, TheMealDB may receive your IP address and standard technical information from your device. How that data is handled is governed by TheMealDB's own policies, which we do not control.

4. Permissions

The App only uses the INTERNET permission, to load recipe data and images from TheMealDB.

5. Ads, analytics, and tracking

The App shows no ads and uses no third-party analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting services. We do not sell or share user data.

6. Security

Requests to TheMealDB use HTTPS. Planner data is kept in the App's private storage on your device.

7. Children's privacy

The App does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

8. Changes to this policy

If the App changes in a way that affects how data is handled (for example, adding accounts, ads, or analytics), this policy will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change.
